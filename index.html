Revenue Splitter

v1.1.0 · WooCommerce · GPL-2.0-or-laterstable

Sales reporting with automatic per-product VAT extraction and dynamic revenue distribution among beneficiaries — authors, publishers, collaborators. Configure global defaults once, override per product, and track every beneficiary's net earnings across all products for any period.

  • Per-product VAT override with global default (gross × rate ÷ (100 + rate))
  • Beneficiary percentage splits with live Σ = 100% validation
  • Filter reports by product or beneficiary, CSV export (UTF-8 BOM)
  • Bilingual UI (Ελληνικά / English), per-user
  • HPOS-compatible — no legacy order tables touched
