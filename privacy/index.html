This website

noxpress.tech has no cookies, no analytics, no ads and no other tracking. Fonts and images load from the site itself.

  • Hosting: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like every web server, GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses to keep the service secure. We have no access to those logs.
  • Storage in your browser: the site keeps the language you chose (nx_lang) in localStorage, and when you last saw the support note in the download window (nx_dl_support_seen, nx_dl_support_off), so it does not show again for 30 days. These stay on your device and are never sent anywhere. Clear them at any time from your browser settings.
  • Download counter: the number of downloads adds up the totals GitHub gives for each file. It is not about people and does not record who downloaded what.

The plugins on your site

noxpress plugins run on your own WordPress. Your customers’ data stays on your server and you are responsible for it. We never see or receive it.

  • Update check: every 12 hours the noxpress Hub reads noxpress.tech/updates.json and downloads updates from GitHub. Like every WordPress request, it carries the WordPress version and your site’s address in the User-Agent. We do not record or see them. A coming Hub release will send only “Noxpress-Hub/version”.
  • No other contact with us: the plugins have no telemetry, never “phone home” and have no licence checks. The ones that connect to other services (for example Viber, Dropbox, orOS) do so only after you set them up, directly to those services.
  • Privacy tools: every plugin that keeps personal data works with WordPress’s “Export / Erase Personal Data” tools, and adds its text to the privacy policy guide, so you can complete your own page.

Android apps

The apps have no account, no analytics, no ads and no third-party libraries that send data.

  • Site Bridge app: talks only to the noxpress Hub of your own site. The device keys stay in the Android Keystore and never leave the phone. The camera is used only to scan ISBNs, on the phone. Every 12 hours it checks GitHub Releases for a new version, unless you installed it from F-Droid or Obtainium.
  • Live Operator app: opens only the site you connected. What it shows comes from there, under that site’s privacy policy.
